# Guitar practice app: store reviews, October (iPhone and Android)

The app: a metronome, chord and scale drills, a practice log, and a tab importer. Subscription price went from 3.99 to 5.99 a month on Oct 1. A left-handed mode exists under Settings → Display → Fretboard orientation.
