# Plant-care app: user interview, Oct 22 (40 min, video call)

## What the app does today

Plant library with care guides; add your plants; watering schedule per plant with reminders (reminder time can be changed in Settings → Reminders); identify a plant from a photo; household sharing (Settings → Household) so several people can see and tick off the same plants.

## Participant

Uses the app for 8 months, 14 plants, lives with a partner, travels for work about one week a month.

## Transcript (excerpt)

1. **Interviewer:** How do you use the app on a normal week?
2. **P:** Mostly the reminders. I open it when it buzzes, water, tick it off.
3. **P:** Honestly the 7 a.m. buzz is annoying. I'm not watering plants at 7 a.m. I just swipe it away and forget.
4. **Interviewer:** What happens when you travel?
5. **P:** That's the worst part. My partner waters them but they don't have the app, so I text them a list from my phone every time. Half the time they water the cactus too much.
6. **P:** I wish I could just give them the list in the app or something.
7. **Interviewer:** Would you like a feature that tells you why a plant looks unhealthy?
8. **P:** Oh, yes, definitely, that would be great.
9. **P:** Last month my fiddle leaf got yellow leaves and I had no idea. I searched online for an hour. I ended up moving it away from the window. No idea if that was right.
10. **P:** The photo thing got my pothos wrong, it said it was a philodendron. So I don't really trust it now.
11. **Interviewer:** Anything else you'd change?
12. **P:** Dark mode maybe? Not a big deal.
13. **P:** Oh and I'd pay for, like, a plant doctor. A real person who looks at a photo. Maybe five bucks.
14. **P:** And when I water, I never know how much. The app says "water" but is that a glass? A litre?
15. **Interviewer:** Thanks, that's really helpful.
