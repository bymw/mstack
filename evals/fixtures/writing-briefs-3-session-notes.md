# Team calendar: new scheduling flow, notes for the leadership session

Session: leadership group, next Tuesday, 25 minutes. They decide whether the new scheduling flow ships to all teams this quarter. They have seen the problem recap ("scheduling takes too many steps") in each of the last two sessions.

## Decided
- Scheduling goes from five screens to two: pick people and a time, then confirm.
- Suggested times appear first, based on everyone's free time.
- The old flow stays available behind "More options" for one release.

## Evidence
- Moderated test, 6 participants: 5 of 6 scheduled faster with the new flow. (Research report, 9 October.)
- A product analyst said the prototype "should cut reschedules by about 30%". Not measured; based on one internal pilot team.

## What we need from leadership
- A yes to ship to all teams this quarter.

## Open questions
- Does the suggested-times feature belong on the free plan or the paid plan?
- Should we import availability from other calendar apps in this release or the next?
- Naming: "Find a time" or "Schedule"?
