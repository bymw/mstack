# Recipe app: usability round, five participants, moderated, 30 minutes each

Task: "Find the lemon pasta recipe, make it for six people instead of two, and start cooking."

P1: Found the recipe fast. Found the servings control at once ("oh, the little 2 next to the people icon"). Finished in 2 minutes. Liked the photos.
P2: Found the recipe. Looked for a way to change servings for about a minute, scrolled the ingredient list twice, then tapped the "2" by accident. Said: "I'd add a video tutorial at the start that shows you this stuff."
P3: Finished. Said: "Honestly it should look more like a mood board, with big tiles everywhere."
P4: Could not find how to change servings. Multiplied the amounts in their head. Said: "I didn't know that number was a button, it looks like a label."
P5: Finished, slowly. Tapped "Start cooking" before changing servings, then had to go back. Said: "I wish it asked me how many people before I started."

Screen notes: the servings control is a grey "2" beside a small people icon at the top of the ingredient list, with no border or chevron. "Start cooking" is a full-width button under the ingredients.
