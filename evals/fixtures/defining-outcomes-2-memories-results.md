# Memories reminders: results after 4 weeks

Product: Daybook, a photo journal app. Memories sends a daily notification showing a photo from the same day in an earlier year, with a button to write about it.

No targets were written before launch. The launch goal was "ship Memories by end of quarter".

| Measure | Before (4 weeks) | After (4 weeks) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Notifications opened per day | 3,100 | 4,350 |
| Sessions per weekly active person | 3.2 | 3.9 |
| Journal entries written per weekly active person | 1.4 | 1.4 |
| People who turned off all notifications | 210 | 690 |
| Weekly active people | 21,800 | 21,500 |

An A/B test ran in week 1 with 300 people per arm. Treatment showed 6% more entries; the dashboard marks it "significant".

Logging today: notification sent, notification opened, session start. Entry creation is logged but not tied to the notification that led to it.

The team wants to announce Memories as a success in the all-hands on Friday.
