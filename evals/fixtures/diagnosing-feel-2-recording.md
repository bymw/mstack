Product: Simmer, a recipe app (web), Safari on iPad Air, landscape, 1180 x 820.
Clip: tabs-flash.mov, 60 fps. Gesture: tap from "Saved" to "Recent", then back to "Saved".
Both tabs had already loaded once before the recording started.
