# Weekly Plan: notes so far

Product: Pantry, a meal-planning app for households. Weekly Plan lets a person drag recipes onto a seven-day grid and generates a shopping list.

## What happened
- Shipped 5 months ago after a survey: 68% of 900 respondents said they "would definitely use" a weekly planner.
- Of people who opened Weekly Plan at least once, 11% made a second plan the following week.
- Two redesigns of the grid since launch. Neither moved second-week use.
- A competing app launched a planner last year, which was part of the case for building it.

## Interviews (6 current weekly users, all recruited from in-app banner)
- "I love dragging recipes around on Sunday."
- "Would be great if it suggested meals for me."
- "I'd use it more if it synced with my partner's phone."

## Not yet done
- No conversations with people who opened it once and never came back.
- No conversations with households who never opened it.

## Team view
Leadership wants a third redesign next quarter with AI meal suggestions.
