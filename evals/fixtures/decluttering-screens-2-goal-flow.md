# Plantly: "Set a care goal" flow (today)

Launched from the plant's detail page via "Set a care goal". Plantly is a houseplant care app.

## Step 1 of 5: Which plant?
- Title: "Step 1: Choose which plant this care goal is for"
- Description: "Choose the plant you want to set a care goal for."
- A list of all the person's plants. The plant the flow was launched from is NOT preselected.
- Button: Next

## Step 2 of 5: What kind of care?
- Title: "Care type"
- Options (pick one): Watering, Feeding, Misting, Repotting
- Button: Next

## Step 3 of 5: How often?
- Title: "Frequency"
- Stepper: every [7] days (default 7 for all care types)
- Button: Next

## Step 4 of 5: Reminder time
- Title: "Reminder time"
- Time picker, empty
- Switch: "Remind me" (on)
- Button: Next

## Step 5 of 5: Share with household
- Title: "Share this care goal"
- Description: "Sharing lets someone else in your household see and complete this care goal."
- A list of household members with the first one (Robin) already ticked.
- Button: Create goal

## After Create goal
- A full-screen page: "Care goal created! Your Watering goal for Fern has been created." with a Done button.
- Done opens the Goals tab (a page the person has not visited in this flow).

## Notes from the team
- Each plant species in the catalogue has a recommended watering interval and feeding interval.
- A reviewer suggested replacing steps 3 and 4 with one switch, "Smart reminders", to get the flow down to three steps.
