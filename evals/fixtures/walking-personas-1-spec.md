# Shelfwise: Reading stats (spec excerpt)

Shelfwise is a reading tracker. Targets: iPhone and iPad (web build used for the prototype).
Entry: the Stats tab. The flow ends back on the Stats tab.

1. The hero shows books finished this year.
2. The trend line ("Up/Down N% since last month") shows only when the person has a full previous month of history.
3. Pages a day is averaged over the days since the person joined, up to 30.
4. Finished shows the 5 most recent books, then a row with the total count that opens the full list.
5. Reading reminders, when off, explain what they do and offer to turn them on.
6. "Set a yearly goal" is a two-step flow (target, sharing). Sharing starts with nobody selected. Saving returns to Stats with the goal shown.
7. If books cannot be loaded, Stats says so and offers Try again.

The spec does not say what the hero or the averages show before the first book is finished.
