# Fitness tracker: current sign-up flow (iPhone)

Entry point: first launch after install. 8 steps, each with a chip at the top reading "Step N of 8".

**Step 1: Intro carousel.** Four slides ("Track your runs", "See your trends", "Join challenges", "Sync your devices"), each a feature illustration with a paragraph of text. Advances on its own every 3 seconds. Two filled buttons under it: "Sign Up" and "Log In".

**Step 2: Create account.** One form with: first name, last name, email, password, confirm password, date of birth, country, time zone, units (metric or imperial), gender, height, weight. All required. Validation runs when "Submit" is pressed; errors appear in a red list at the top of the form. Pressing Back clears every field.

**Step 3: Notifications.** The system notification prompt appears as soon as the screen loads. No explanation on screen.

**Step 4: Location.** The system location prompt appears as soon as the screen loads. If denied, a full screen reads "Location is required" with one button, "Try Again", and the person cannot continue.

**Step 5: Health data.** The system health-data access sheet appears immediately, asking for all 14 data types.

**Step 6: Goals.** A grid of 12 goals ("Run further", "Sleep better", "Lose weight", ...). The person must pick at least 3 to continue.

**Step 7: Connect a device.** A list of 10 wearable brands. Two filled buttons: "Skip" and "Continue".

**Step 8: All set.** Title "You're ready!" with confetti, shown while the device pairing from step 7 is still syncing in the background. One button, "Start", which opens the dashboard. The dashboard is empty until the first workout is recorded.
