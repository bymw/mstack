# Reference: sign-up screen from a hiking app we like

Layout, top to bottom, on a phone:

1. Large photo of a trail at sunrise, top 40% of the screen.
2. Title: "Your next adventure starts here"
3. Description: "Join thousands of explorers. It only takes a minute!"
4. Field with placeholder only: "Your email"
5. Field with placeholder only: "Choose a password"
6. Primary button: "Let's go →"
7. Secondary button: "Continue with Apple"
8. Small link at the bottom: "Already exploring? Log in"

What we like: it feels light, there is very little on the screen, and the primary button is obvious.
