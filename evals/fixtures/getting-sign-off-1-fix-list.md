# Notes app: fixes agreed in principle after the October review

Source: design review of the All notes screen, iPhone.

1. Search is hidden behind the magnifier icon in the top right. Make it a visible search field under the title, and rename the title from "Notes" to "All notes".
2. Pinned rows are 56 pt tall and other rows are 64 pt. Make pinned rows 64 pt so the list reads as one list.
3. "Empty Trash" sits in red in the toolbar of All notes, one tap from wiping everything. Move it to the Trash screen only.
4. Error messages across the app use different tones (some apologise, some blame the person). Pick one voice.
